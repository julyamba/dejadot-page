How it works

Your only enemy is what you just did.

Every round is five seconds long. The route you take to the coin becomes the next thing you have to avoid.

Grab

A coin appears somewhere in the arena. Drag anywhere on the screen to steer your dot to it before the timer bar runs out.

Echo

Clear the round and your exact path replays as an echo in the next one. Faint lines show where every echo is going to travel.

Survive

One more echo joins each round. Take the coin without touching any of them, then stay clear until the five seconds are up.

Features

Small game. Nothing in the way.

No sign-in, no ads, no setup. Open it and play.

A daily challenge

One run a day with the same coin positions for every player. Only your first attempt is scored, then it turns into practice. Keep a streak going by coming back tomorrow.

Drag anywhere to steer

Steering is relative, so your thumb never has to cover the dot. The dot stops the moment your finger does, and five sensitivity levels let you tune how far it travels.

Six themes to unlock

Reach 5, 10, 15, 20 and 30 coins in one run to unlock new colours and trail styles.

Stats and achievements

Best scores, averages, close calls, your last 30 runs, and 12 achievements to chase.

Close calls count

Skim past an echo and the game notices, with a tick you can feel and a tally in your stats.

Sound and haptics

Synth effects with a coin chime that climbs in pitch as your score grows. Both can be switched off.

Private by design

No ads, no analytics and no internet access. The only permission it asks for is vibration.

Pauses when life interrupts

Leave the app mid-round and the run waits for you, with a 3-2-1 countdown when you return.

Good to know

Straight answers.

Echo is a small offline game. Here is what it does and does not do.

Does it need internet or an account?

No. There is no sign-in and the app has no internet access at all. Everything stays on your phone.

Are there ads or purchases?

No ads and nothing to buy.

Is the daily challenge really the same for everyone?

Yes. The coin positions come from the date, so every player gets the same sequence that day. Scores are not compared online; it is your own streak and your own best.

Why did I lose while standing still?

Your last echo ends exactly where you finished the previous round, which is where you are standing. Echoes are harmless for the first moments of a round, shown as dashed rings, so use that time to move.

What happens to my progress if I uninstall?

Scores, unlocks and settings are stored only on the phone. Uninstalling or switching phones can lose them unless Android's own app backup restores them.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not at the moment. Echo is Android only.
