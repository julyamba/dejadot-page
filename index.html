Round 01

Five seconds. One coin.

A coin appears somewhere in the arena. Drag anywhere on the screen to steer your dot to it before the bar at the top runs out.

That is the whole first round. It is meant to feel easy.

Round 02

Then your past shows up.

The exact path you just took replays as an echo. Same route, same timing, same hesitation at the corner.

Echoes are harmless for the first moments of a round, drawn as dashed rings. After that, touching one ends the run.

Round 03

And it never leaves.

Every round you clear adds one more echo. By round ten you are threading through nine copies of yourself, and each careless detour you ever made is still out there.

Faint lines show where every echo will travel, so the skill is planning a route that your future self can live with.

What's inside

Small on purpose.

01

Daily challenge

One run a day with the same coin positions for every player. Your first attempt is scored, the rest are practice, and a streak counts the days you show up.

02

Drag-anywhere steering

Steering is relative, so your thumb never covers the dot, and the dot stops when your finger does. Five sensitivity levels.

03

Six themes

Unlocked at 5, 10, 15, 20 and 30 coins in a single run, each with its own colours and trail style.

04

Stats and achievements

Best scores, averages, close calls, your last 30 runs, and 12 achievements.

05

Sound and haptics

Synth effects with a coin chime that climbs in pitch as your score grows. Both can be switched off.

06

Pause that waits

Leave the app mid-round and the run holds, then counts 3-2-1 when you come back.

07

Nothing else

No ads, no analytics, no sign-in and no internet access. The only permission is vibration.

Questions

Straight answers.

Does it need internet or an account?
No. There is no sign-in and the app has no internet access. Everything stays on your phone.
Are there ads or purchases?
No ads and nothing to buy.
Is the daily really the same for everyone?
Yes. The coin positions come from the date. Scores are not compared online; it is your own streak and your own best.
Why did I lose while standing still?
Your newest echo ends exactly where you finished the last round, which is where you are standing. Move during the dashed-ring moments.
What if I uninstall?
Progress is stored only on the phone, so it can be lost unless Android's own app backup restores it.
Is there an iPhone version?
Not at the moment. Echo is Android only.
